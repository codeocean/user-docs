# Queue Time & Compute Environment Utilization

## Queue Time

Prior to starting a run, users may find it helpful to check the current Queue Time, located at the top right corner of the Pipelines IDE.&#x20;

Queue Time offers insight into how long their Pipeline may wait prior to beginning execution, by showing the average wait time for recently started tasks across all running Pipelines in the deployment.
